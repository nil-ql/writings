Backend Content Workflow

Use this page as the content authoring workflow. The homepage is intentionally minimal. New papers should be added by creating a new HTML page, not by editing the home page layout.

Step 1: Create a new post page

Open post-template.html. Save a copy with a clear filename like paper-4.html.

Step 2: Add your paper content

Replace the placeholder title, date, summary, and full content in the copied page. Keep the format consistent with the example.

Step 3: Register the post on the homepage

After the new page exists, add a new entry to index.html inside the post list:

<li class="post">
  <h2><a href="paper-4.html">Your Paper Title</a></h2>
  <time datetime="2026-05-16">May 16, 2026</time>
  <p>One-line description of the research and what it shows.</p>
  <a href="paper-4.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Read full post</a>
</li>

Step 4: Keep the homepage clean

Do not add post creation forms or extra cards on the homepage. The site should remain a clean, black-text research archive with links to dedicated post pages.

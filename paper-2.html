Distributed Systems Resilience through Predictive Scheduling

July 18, 2025

A systems-level study on workload prediction and autonomous recovery strategies for cloud-native infrastructure.

This research presents a predictive scheduling layer for distributed clusters, allowing systems to anticipate load spikes and proactively allocate resources. The model reduces cascading failures by integrating historical patterns with live telemetry.

By focusing on resilience and service continuity, the work delivers a blueprint for production-grade scheduling systems that stay stable even under dynamic workloads.
